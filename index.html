Good questions. Straight answers.

Who do you work with?

Independent, full-service restaurants, the kind where the food and the room both matter. Right now we serve restaurants, bars and lounges across the Rio Grande Valley: McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Pharr and Weslaco. Outside the Valley? Reach out anyway. We're open to growing, and we'd love to hear about your place.

What does a restaurant marketing agency do?

Everything that gets guests through your door. With Palate that means strategy, content production (photography, short-form video and graphic design), social media management, Meta and Google ads, web design, and consulting. One team, one monthly program, so you're not managing five freelancers.

How can I increase sales at my restaurant?

Give people a reason to come in on the nights that need it. Upgrade an offer you already own, theme the night so it feels like an event, then put paid ads behind it 24 to 36 hours before doors open. That's how Sip n' Tell turned a $1,300 Taco Tuesday into a $4,500 one, and how LUMA's themed brunches went from $1,100 to over $5,000. Keep posting daily so every new guest finds a page that looks alive. Read the full guide →

Do Facebook and Instagram ads work for restaurants?

Yes, when every ad points at one clear action. LUMA spent $1,200 on Meta ads in its first month and booked 80+ reservations straight from the campaign through OpenTable, about $15 a table. Smokin' Moon BBQ gained 5,100+ followers in 30 days on a $500 budget.

Which restaurants have you worked with?

Santa Fe Steakhouse & Cantina, LUMA Cocktail Lounge, Sip n' Tell, El Taco Mañanero, Howling Rabbits Beerworks, Smokin' Moon BBQ & Beer Garden, The Quarter, Kiss & Fly, The Fifth Cocktail Lounge, University Draft House, The Wings Stadium, McAllen Country Club, Monzi Social Events Center and more.

What's included?

Everything, every month. The Full House Program covers strategy, up to 30 posts, graphic design, photography, short-form video, Meta and Google ads, community management, and your website. You also keep the playbook: our SOPs, scripts and templates, ready to share with your staff. No menu to order from and no add-ons to approve.

How fast do we get started?

Fast. Once you say yes, we'll have your onboarding call on the calendar within 24 to 48 hours. That's where we get to know your restaurant. Your first content and ads can be live in as little as seven days.

When does billing start?

Not until your work is out in the world. Your billing starts the day your first post and your first ad go live. Until then, you don't pay a thing.

Is there a contract?

Yes, and it's a simple one. We ask for a three-month minimum to start, which gives us time to learn your restaurant and build momentum. After that, it's month to month. If you ever want to stop, just give us 30 days' notice. Some of our clients have been with us for four years.

How much does restaurant marketing cost?

One monthly price covers the whole program. We'd rather walk you through it than put a number on a page. On a 45-minute strategy call, we'll get to know your room first, then show you exactly what it costs and what you get.
