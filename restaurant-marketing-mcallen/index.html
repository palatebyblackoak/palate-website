Restaurant marketing in McAllen and the Rio Grande Valley.

What works for restaurants in the Valley, what it can do, and how to pick the right team.

By Luis Alonso Robles, founder of Palate · Updated November 2026 · 6-min read

The short answer

Restaurant marketing in McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Pharr and Weslaco works best when one team runs all of it:

  1. Strategy built around your slow nights and your calendar.
  2. Content production: photos, short-form video and design shot at your restaurant.
  3. Social media management that posts consistently and answers your guests.
  4. Meta and Google ads pointed at reservations and visits, not likes.
  5. A website that makes booking or ordering one tap away.
  6. Consulting: a monthly sit-down to read the numbers and plan what's next.

The Valley eats out. That's the good news.

The hard part is that your guests have more choices every year, and most of them decide where to go on their phone, scrolling Instagram and Facebook on the way home from work. If your restaurant isn't showing up there, with food that looks as good as it tastes, they pick somewhere that is.

Here's what restaurant marketing in the RGV should look like, what it can do, and how to pick the right team for it.

01Why restaurant marketing in the Valley is its own thing

National playbooks miss what makes the RGV different. A few things we've learned working with restaurants here:

  • Your city is a small world. McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Pharr and Weslaco blend together. A guest in Weslaco will drive to McAllen for the right night. Ads should reach the whole Valley, not just your zip code.
  • Word of mouth moves online. The friend who tells everyone about a great dinner now does it in a story. Give them something worth posting.
  • Local voices carry weight. The creators your guests already follow can send more people through the door than a month of your own posts.
  • Speak the way your guests speak. English, Spanish or both. Your content should sound like your dining room, not a corporate chain.

03Someone should be in your restaurant every month

Stock photos and phone pictures from the kitchen don't fill tables. Your food, your room and your staff do.

That's why we come to you. Once a month we show up at your restaurant for a media day and shoot the photos and video for the month ahead. Then we sit down with you, walk through the numbers and plan what's next. Like any good regular, we keep coming back.

You perfect the plate. We carry the word.

04How to choose a restaurant marketing agency in McAllen

Before you sign with anyone, ask these questions:

  • Do you only work with restaurants? A team that also markets dentists and car washes will treat your Tuesday like any other ad.
  • Can you show revenue, not just followers? Followers are nice. A $1,300 night turning into a $4,500 night pays the bills.
  • Will you come to my restaurant? If the content isn't shot on-site, it won't look like your place.
  • Do the same people run my content and my ads? When two vendors split the work, nobody owns the result.
  • What does the contract look like? Ask about the minimum term, how to cancel and when billing starts.

For the record, here's how we answer that last one: a three-month minimum to start, then month to month with 30 days' notice. Billing starts the day your first post and first ad go live, not before.
